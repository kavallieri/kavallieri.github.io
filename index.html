What is Hack Club?

What is
Hack Club?

Hack Club is the world's largest nonprofit movement of teenagers making cool projects.

Hack Club empowers teens to actually create.
Our community is based on the simple concept of making, learning, and getting good at
what you love.

You can learn more on Hack Club's
philosophy page.

Kavallieri Hack Club

Kavallieri
Hack Club

Kavallieri Hack Club is basically a branch of the international organisation.

We are Malta's first ever Hack Club, founded in late August 2026, and physically based in G.F. Abela Junior College
